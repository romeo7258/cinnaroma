About D PixelTactile Fims and Design

We're on a mission to democratize high-quality education and empower the next generation of creators and technologists.

Our Mission

Our mission is to nurture world-class creative talent by providing affordable, high-impact, and flexible learning experiences. We are committed to equipping our students with cutting-edge software skills, studio-tested workflows, and the confidence to turn their creative vision into a successful global career.

Quick Overview

D PixelTactile Films and Design is a premier online education platform dedicated to bridging the gap between traditional education and industry demands. It creates high-impact, industry-aligned training in visual arts, post-production, and interactive media. Designed for aspiring artists, designers, and career switchers, our curriculum bridges the gap between creative passion and professional studio standards.
