3D Modeling in Maya

3D Modeling in Maya is the process of shaping and building digital 3D objects, characters, and environments inside a virtual space. Think of it like working with digital clay, where creators can craft anything from simple props to detailed movie characters and futuristic worlds. It serves as the foundation for modern animation, special effects, and video games, giving artists full creative freedom to turn imaginary ideas into tangible, realistic 3D visuals.

What You Will Learn

Navigate Autodesk Maya’s workspace and master fundamental polygon modeling tools like Extrude, Bevel, and Multi-Cut.

Build detailed hard-surface models, sci-fi props, and complex mechanical parts with clean edge loops and supporting geometry.

Harness curves and NURBS surfaces to model organic pipes, cables, and smooth surface profiles.

Design modular 3D environments, architecture, and props following industry scale and grid standards.

Sculpt complete 3D characters, facial features, hair, and clothing with production-ready topology and edge flow.

Optimize low-poly geometry using Quad Draw retopology and set up clay turntables for portfolio presentation.
