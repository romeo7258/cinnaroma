Print Media Design with Illustrator and Indesign with AI

Adobe Illustrator is a drawing program made for creating custom images and graphics from scratch. Think of it like a digital drawing board where everything you draw is made of smooth lines instead of square dots. Because of this, you can shrink a drawing down to fit on a postage stamp or blow it up to cover a massive billboard, and it will stay perfectly sharp without ever getting blurry. People use it to design logos, icons, cartoon characters, and unique lettering.

Adobe InDesign, on the other hand, is a page-setup program made for putting together finished documents, especially ones with lots of pages and text. Instead of drawing shapes, you use it to arrange photos, illustrations, and long articles on a page. It is built to handle things like magazines, books, brochures, and reports. It makes it easy to add page numbers automatically, keep your text looking uniform, and organize everything so a multi-page document looks clean and easy to read.
