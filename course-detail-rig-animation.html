3ds Max & Maya Rigging & Animation

3ds Max & Maya Rigging & Animation is the art of bringing digital 3D models to life, turning static objects and characters into fluid, believable actors. Rigging works like constructing an invisible skeleton inside a 3D model, allowing artists to pose, bend, and move it with control and flexibility. Once rigged, animation gives these models motion and personality—whether it is a character walking, talking, and expressing emotion, or a complex machine coming to life with precise movement. It is the essential magic behind animated movies, video games, and dynamic visual presentations.

What You Will Learn

Construct production-ready character rigs, joint hierarchies, constraints, and custom FK/IK control systems for flexible posing.

Bind 3D mesh geometry seamlessly to skeletons, fine-tune skin weight painting, and apply corrective shape keys for clean mesh deformation.

Build expressively detailed facial rigs using blendshapes, set-driven keys, and functional rigging setups for mechanical and hard-surface props.

Master timing, spacing, squash and stretch, graph editors, and weight balance across bouncing balls, pendulums, and physical interactions.

Animate full-body character movements, including realistic walking, running, jumping, parkour vaults, and quadruped locomotion cycles.

Craft believable character performances with facial expressions, audio lip-syncing, two-character interactions, and cinematic camera layout.
